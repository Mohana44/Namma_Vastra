# Namma Vastra 🧵

**Namma Vastra** is a professional digital ecosystem designed to empower the traditional weavers of Karnataka, specifically those crafting the legendary **Ilkal** and **Molkalmuru** sarees. 

Our platform bridges the gap between ancient heritage and the modern global market, providing artisans with the tools they need to showcase their work, calculate fair prices, and stay ahead of fashion trends.

---

## 🚀 Key Vision & Goals

*   **Our Vision**: To preserve and promote the rich handloom heritage of Karnataka, ensuring every weaver's craft is recognized and valued globally.
*   **Our Mission**: Empowering local artisans by providing them with direct access to modern markets, fair pricing tools, and design insights.
*   **Our Goal**: To connect every weaver to the world, bridging the gap between traditional craftsmanship and the global market.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏛️ Heritage Collections
Direct insight into the storytelling and techniques behind **Ilkal** and **Molkalmuru** silks.

### 📈 Real-time Trend Board
A professional dashboard for weavers to track trending color palettes and high-demand patterns (Tope Teni, Chokki Paras, Temple Borders, etc.) for the current month.

### 💰 Fair Price Calculator
A sophisticated tool that helps artisans calculate the ideal retail price based on material costs, labor, and time, ensuring they receive the compensation they deserve.

### 🖼️ Loom Gallery
A digital showroom where weavers can upload their latest creations. Each piece is linked to a direct inquiry system (WhatsApp integration) for potential buyers and boutiques.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

*   **Language**: Kotlin
*   **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose (Modern, Declarative UI)
*   **Database**: Google Firebase Firestore (Real-time data synchronization)
*   **Storage**: Firebase Storage (Secure image hosting)
*   **Architecture**: Modern Android MVVM principles
